The simulation results indicate that the strategic future of DBS Treasures lies not in the total replacement of human intervention with digital tools, but in the precision-timed integration of both. To maintain the predicted **74% Net Positive Macro Sentiment**, the organization must transition from a "functional" digital model to a "context-aware" hybrid ecosystem. The primary strategic implication is that the **S$350,000 AUM threshold** acts as more than a financial requirement; it is a psychological boundary where users demand a shift from automated execution to validated advisory.

**Defensive Recommendations: Mitigating the 'Validation Pivot'**

The simulation identifies a critical risk point when users attempt high-value transactions within the Digibank app. The "Validation Pivot" occurs when a user halts a digital process to seek human reassurance, often triggered by the complexity of the "DBS Treasures digital architecture" which "embeds global market trading and institutional research into a single platform."

> “I very much like the efficiency of Digibank, but when I’m about to commit S$500,000 to a new fund, I need my RM to confirm that my risk profile is truly aligned. If the app just shows a loading spinner, I feel a sudden surge of anxiety.”

To defend against this friction, DBS must implement **Real-Time Status Mirroring**. This allows an RM to see the exact screen and error state a Treasures client is viewing. By reducing the "explanation friction," the bank can prevent the 12% NPS drop predicted in the simulation for clients exceeding the S$500,000 AUM mark who feel "digitally abandoned" during complex maneuvers.

**Tactical Action Plan for Corporate Implementation**

The following actionable steps are derived directly from the simulation's "Success Path" data and the "DBS Digibank Navigation Guide" mechanics:

- **Implement "Friction-Sensing" Triggers:** Configure Digibank to alert the assigned RM if a Treasures client spends more than 120 seconds on a complex investment page without proceeding.
- **Deploy Co-Browsing Capabilities:** Enable a secure, one-way "view-only" mode for RMs to assist clients in navigating the app during high-stakes transactions.
- **Context-Aware "Call RM" Button:** Replace the generic support hotline with a smart button that transmits the user's current digital context to the RM’s dashboard before the call connects.

> “If the app could tell me that this suggestion is based on the risk profile my RM and I discussed last month, I would be much more willing to hit 'Confirm' without calling anyone.”

**Governance and Evolution Monitoring**

The simulation warns that if the "Human-Digital Friction" is not addressed, the bank faces a "Net-Negative Pivot" among the 55-70 age cohort within 18 months. DBS should establish a **Hybrid NPS Metric** that measures the speed of transition from an App-based error or hesitation to a human-led resolution. This metric should be the primary KPI for the Treasures digital team, ensuring that the "Live more, Bank less" philosophy remains a reality even as asset complexity increases beyond the S$350,000 baseline.
