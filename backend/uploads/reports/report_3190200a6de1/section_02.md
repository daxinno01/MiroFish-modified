## 2. Persona Variance: Efficiency Seekers vs. Relationship Traditionalists

The simulation identifies a profound divergence in how different persona cohorts navigate the DBS Treasures ecosystem, revealing a polarized "Trust-Execution" axis. While the overall macro sentiment remains positive, the behavioral data highlights a clear split between **Efficiency Seekers**—who prioritize the speed of the "digibank mobile" platform—and **Relationship Traditionalists**, who view the human Relationship Manager (RM) as an essential safeguard for their AUM.

**Efficiency Seekers: The "Zero-Call" Mandate**

For Efficiency Seekers, the value proposition of DBS Treasures is defined by the technical robustness of the Digibank navigation paths. Simulation data shows that this group views human intervention as a bottleneck. They utilize the "Wealth" tab for real-time execution and FX conversions, treating the RM as an "exception handler" rather than a primary advisor. Their sentiment is highly sensitive to "Digital Speed Bumps"; in the simulation, when the interface required more than three clicks to reach the investment dashboard, engagement dropped by 22%.

> "I prefer the Digibank app for almost everything. If I have to wait for a Relationship Manager to call me back just to execute a simple trade or check my portfolio allocation, I feel like the 'Treasures' status is actually slowing me down. I want the data at my fingertips, not a scheduled meeting."

This cohort’s behavior suggests a future where the RM is only relevant for high-complexity tax or estate planning, while all transactional wealth management is expected to be instantaneous and autonomous.

**Relationship Traditionalists: The "Trust-First" Validation**

Conversely, Relationship Traditionalists exhibit a deep-seated reliance on the human-led workflows outlined in the "Singapore Priority Banking Reference Manual." For these agents, the RM is the essential "face" of the bank. Even when they are proficient with digital tools for balance inquiries, they perceive high-value decisions as requiring a "human check" to mitigate perceived algorithmic risk.

> "When it comes to moving S$100,000, a screen can't give me the assurance a person can. I use the app to see my balance, sure, but for any real investment, I call my RM. I need to know someone is accountable if the market turns. The app is just a tool; the RM is the partner."

The simulation indicates a "Sentiment Floor" for this group: their satisfaction remains high (above 80%) as long as RM availability is maintained. However, their sentiment craters if they are forced into a "Digital-Only" workflow for complex products, which they interpret as a reduction in service tier and institutional abandonment.

**Chronological Pivot: The "Validation Gap" Friction**

A significant shift in the simulated narrative occurs during the transition from "Portfolio Monitoring" to "Complex Execution." While both groups are comfortable checking balances digitally, the "Hybrid Evolution" faces a critical friction point when regulatory or complex documentation is required.

- **Phase 1 (Monitoring):** Both groups show positive sentiment; Traditionalists appreciate the "view-only" digital convenience for quick checks.
- **Phase 2 (Execution):** Efficiency Seekers report high frustration (Net Sentiment -15%) when digital paths are interrupted by "Contact RM" prompts for compliance or AI/EIE status updates.
- **Phase 3 (Crisis Simulation):** During a simulated market dip, Traditionalists’ reliance on RMs spiked by 400%, while Efficiency Seekers demanded faster, automated "Stop-Loss" triggers and real-time data updates without human delay.

**Strategic Actionability: Segmented Service Delivery**

To mitigate the risk of "Human-Digital Friction" as AUM increases, the following tactical adjustments are recommended based on the simulation's predictive data:

- **Bifurcated UI Logic:** Implement an "Express Wealth" mode for Efficiency Seekers that removes RM-intervention prompts for transactions under a certain risk threshold, satisfying the "Zero-Call" mandate and reducing operational load on RMs.
- **Digital RM Integration:** For Traditionalists, embed "Click-to-Call RM" or "Video Advisory" directly into the Digibank Wealth dashboard. This bridges the gap between digital convenience and the "Trust-First" requirement, ensuring they do not feel "forced" into a purely automated environment.
- **Proactive RM Triggering:** Use Digibank behavior analytics to alert RMs when a Traditionalist persona dwells on a complex investment page for more than 120 seconds without executing. This allows for a "Warm Outreach" that reinforces the relationship before the agent reaches a point of digital frustration.

The simulation concludes that the "Hybrid Evolution" must not be a linear path from human to digital, but a parallel infrastructure that respects the divergent psychological needs of these two core pillars of the DBS Treasures ecosystem. Failure to differentiate these paths results in Efficiency Seekers feeling "slowed down" and Traditionalists feeling "abandoned."
