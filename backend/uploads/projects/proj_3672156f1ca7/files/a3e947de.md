# Video Content and Narrative Risk Factors

Specific Visuals:
- The video shows an AI-generated elderly woman accidentally downloading a third-party app to buy cheap durians, leading to her screen being mirrored and funds drained.
- The visuals are highly polished. Thanks to heavy prompt engineering and rigorous human vetting, the main characters and foreground actions are flawless (no six-fingered hands or garbled main text). However, there is one very subtle temporal artifact: at the 1:14 mark, an out-of-focus pedestrian in the background slightly morphs and disappears for a split second. It is barely noticeable to the average viewer but easily caught if analyzed frame-by-frame.

Core Risk Vectors:
- The Irony Risk: The public may fixate on the hypocrisy of using deepfake/GenAI technology to warn the public about scammers who use deepfake/GenAI technology.
- The Empathy Deficit: Because the victim losing her life savings is a synthetic AI avatar, viewers may feel zero emotional resonance, defeating the educational purpose.
- Labor Backlash: High probability of local production houses complaining about lost contracts.
