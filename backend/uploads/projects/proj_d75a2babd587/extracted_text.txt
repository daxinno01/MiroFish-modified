Digital Operational Risk and Innovation in 2026
DBS’s reputation as the "World's Best Digital Bank" has faced renewed challenges in early 2026,
which may influence investor perception of its operational risk profile.
The March 19, 2026 Digital Service Disruption
On March 19, 2026, DBS and POSB customers experienced a disruption to digital banking
services during the lunchtime period, with over 2,100 reports recorded on Downdetector.30 The
outage, which affected app and website access, lasted approximately one hour and was
attributed to a "system change error".30 While the bank restored services by 1:19 pm and
assured customers that funds remained secure, the incident follows a history of digital
disruptions that had previously drawn regulatory action from the MAS.30
The MAS, which had previously imposed a six-month pause on non-essential IT changes (lifted
in April 2024), stated it is following up with the bank to address the root cause of the latest
incident.30 For the prediction engine, this suggests potential for continued regulatory overhead
and higher operational risk capital requirements, which currently stand at 1.8 times the bank's
risk-weighted assets for that category.30
Spark GenAI and the AI Transformation Strategy

To mitigate these risks and drive future efficiency, DBS has accelerated its artificial intelligence
initiatives. On April 23, 2026, the bank announced an enhanced version of its Spark GenAI

to different business maturity levels.34
Within the bank itself, Tan Su Shan has emphasized the expansion of trade finance, payments,
and wealth management through tokenization and generative AI.37 The bank is also in the
process of adding 1,000 new AI-focused roles while reducing contract and temporary staff by
50% over three years to streamline operations.5 This strategic shift toward "industrializing" AI is
a key component of the 2026 outlook, as the bank seeks to improve its cost-to-income ratio,
which currently stands at a healthy 40%.11
