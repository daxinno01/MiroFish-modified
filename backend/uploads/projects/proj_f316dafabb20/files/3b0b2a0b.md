# Media Ecosystem & Editorial Framing

## Reliable / Mainstream Media (The "Authority" Nodes)
These outlets are trusted by the older generation, professionals, and regulators. They will likely frame the campaign around its utility and the technology behind it.

1. **The Straits Times (ST)**
   - **Audience:** Broad national audience, older demographics, government, professionals.
   - **Expected Framing:** Objective, civic-minded. Will focus on the anti-scam initiative and quote DBS executives or the police.
   - **Simulated Headline:** "DBS launches fully AI-generated educational video to combat rising malware scams."
   - **Simulation Role:** Reassures the elderly demographic and provides official legitimacy to the campaign.

2. **CNA (Channel NewsAsia)**
   - **Audience:** Professionals, regional viewers, high-income earners.
   - **Expected Framing:** Focuses on both the civic message and the business/tech angle of using GenAI.
   - **Simulated Headline:** "No human actors: Inside DBS’s push to use GenAI for real-time scam education."

## Viral / Social-First Local Media (The "Amplifier" Nodes)
These sites monitor platforms like Reddit (r/singapore) and TikTok. They will aggressively amplify the memes, the irony, and any public backlash. They are the primary news source for Gen Z and Millennials.

3. **Mothership.sg**
   - **Audience:** Mass youth, Gen Z, Millennials. Highly viral.
   - **Expected Framing:** Snarky, highly attuned to internet culture. Will aggregate Reddit comments and highlight visual AI errors or the "irony" of the campaign.
   - **Simulated Headline:** "DBS made a 100% AI anti-scam video. S'poreans are distracted by the 'uncanny' durian auntie."
   - **Simulation Role:** Bridges the gap between niche Reddit debates and the mainstream public, causing negative sentiment to go viral.

4. **AsiaOne & MUSTShareNews (MSNews)**
   - **Audience:** Heartlanders, general social media users.
   - **Expected Framing:** Sensationalist, focusing on outrage or emotional reactions.
   - **Simulated Headline:** "‘Profit so high but use AI?’: Local creatives slam DBS's new AI-generated scam ad."

## Niche & Cultural Media (The "Think-Piece" Nodes)
These outlets shape the opinions of the intelligentsia, creatives, and industry professionals.

5. **Rice Media**
   - **Audience:** Cultural commentators, creatives, progressives.
   - **Expected Framing:** Long-form critical analysis of the societal implications. Will likely attack the lack of human empathy in an AI ad about losing life savings.
   - **Simulated Headline:** "Can an AI Auntie make us care about scams? The soulless reality of corporate GenAI."
   - **Simulation Role:** Validates the anger of the "Local Creative Professionals" demographic.

6. **Tech in Asia / Vulcan Post**
   - **Audience:** Startup founders, tech workers, corporate strategists.
   - **Expected Framing:** Pragmatic, analyzing the cost-efficiency vs. brand risk.
   - **Simulated Headline:** "DBS’s GenAI gamble: A scalable solution to scam education or a brand risk?"
