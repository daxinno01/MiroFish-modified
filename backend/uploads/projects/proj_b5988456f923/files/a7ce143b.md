# Prior Brand Sentiment & Historical Analogues

Brand Reputation (DBS Bank):
- Trust Level: Very high. DBS is viewed as too-big-to-fail and a staple of Singaporean life.
- Recent Tech Sentiment: While praised for digital innovation, DBS suffered several high-profile digibank app outages in 2023 and 2024. As a result, the public is highly sensitive to DBS's technology choices. There is a lingering narrative of "Fix your servers before spending on fancy AI."
- Anti-Scam Fatigue: Singaporeans are bombarded daily by SMS, print, and digital anti-scam warnings from the police and banks. Standard messaging is often ignored due to fatigue.

Comparable Historical Analogues:
1. Toys "R" Us Sora Ad (2024): 
   - Resulted in massive backlash from creatives calling it "soulless" and "creepy," overshadowing the brand's innovation narrative.
2. Under Armour AI Controversy (2024): 
   - Criticized for taking credit away from human directors.
3. Singapore Police Force (SPF) Anti-Scam Ads:
   - Typically use real, local actors with high dramatic flair (often becoming viral for being "campy" or relatable). An AI video will be directly compared to these beloved human-acted ads.
