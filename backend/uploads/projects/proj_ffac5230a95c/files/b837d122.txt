Ready for a dose of good news? We’re excited to unveil an SGD 10 million support package to help Singaporeans and local SMEs/SMBs tackle rising cost pressures!

From August to December 2026, get ready for over three million cashback redemptions to help you stretch your dollar on everyday essentials like groceries and meals. Our popular SGD 3 cashback every Saturday at hawker stalls and heartland shops will make a comeback – a great way to support both our customers and local businesses.

We're also powering up SMEs/SMBs with enhanced guidance and access to 16,000+ AI solution providers through initiatives like our Spark GenAI programme. This means faster AI implementation to achieve real business value.

This is another step in our journey to stand alongside our community, helping households and uplifting heartland merchants and hawkers who bring so much life and flavour to our neighbourhoods! 🥰

Announcing the support package, Mr Jeffrey Siow, Acting Min for Transport and SMS for Finance, said, “In Singapore, we all pull together to help one another – Government, businesses, and society. This is how we deal with troubles and crisis together. This way we always make sure that no Singaporean is left to bear the burden alone."

DBS Singapore Country Head Lim Him Chuan added, “We hope this gesture of encouragement will go some way in providing practical support to those who need it as we tide over these times together.”

Thank you Mr Siow, who is also MP for Chua Chu Kang GRC, and Dr Choo Pei Ling, MP for Chua Chu Kang GRC, for joining us at Tengah Community Centre for this wonderful occasion.
