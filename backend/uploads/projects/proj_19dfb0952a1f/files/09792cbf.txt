Scams keep changing — so DBS Scam Defence is our multi‑layered approach to keeping customers safe. It brings together:
- education to help you spot scams,
- security tools to safeguard your accounts, and
- robust systems working behind the scenes to defend against rising threats.
When scammers try to rush or pressure you into acting, we build in intentional pauses — moments that help you take a beat and make informed decisions.
One added safeguard is a 12‑hour cooling period, rolled out progressively for higher‑risk account changes like adding a new payee, increasing limits, or updating your contact details. Others include tightening the mobile wallet setup process and introducing customer reverse authentication, which we've rolled out over the past year.
With DBS Scam Defence, your protection always comes first. Together, we can get the upper hand over scammers.
