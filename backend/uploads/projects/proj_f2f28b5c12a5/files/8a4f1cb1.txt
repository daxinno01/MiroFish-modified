The Spark GenAI programme is a strategic initiative by DBS, developed in collaboration with Enterprise Singapore (EnterpriseSG) and the Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA), designed to help SMEs transition from AI curiosity to actual implementation. Recognizing that small businesses often lack the R&D budgets of tech giants, the programme acts as a "tech concierge." It provides a structured three-tier framework—Start, Accelerate, and Scale—tailored to a company's digital maturity. Whether a business is looking for "off-the-shelf" tools to automate customer service or requires bespoke 1-on-1 consultancy for deep backend integration, the programme offers a clear roadmap to reduce the "friction" of adoption.
A standout feature of the programme is its massive ecosystem, granting SMEs direct access to over 16,000 AI solution providers through IMDA’s Open Innovation Platform. To ensure business owners aren't just buying tech for the sake of it, DBS introduced the "Implementing AI for Impact" playbook, which includes diagnostic tools to assess AI readiness. Crucially, the programme is tied to the National AI Strategy 2.0, meaning eligible SMEs can tap into government grants that cover up to 50% of qualifying costs. By combining financial support, expert workshops, and cyber protection coverage, Spark GenAI aims to future-proof the "backbone" of Singapore's economy, ensuring the heartland merchants can leverage the same cutting-edge efficiency as global MNCs.
